## Icon rail and explorer
The far-left rail is always visible (with the SAUS logo on top). The three icons below it are the sections of Apps (AI Image, AI Video, Other)

At the bottom of the rail are the tools: **ComfyUI** (new window), **Files** ([File Manager](FILE_MANAGER.md), as a tab), **Guide** (this guide), **Console** (the ComfyUI log feed, docked over the bottom of the workspace) and **Settings** (as a tab).

The panel is only shown when you open it (Esc closes it), and it remembers the section and the subcategories you had unfolded.
Check out: [App Browsing](APP_BROWSING.md)

## Workspace:
In this section of the screen the apps you opened from the explorer are loaded, each one as a **tab**, so you can jump between several apps without leaving the page. Settings and the File Manager open here as tabs too. A tab can be **enlarged** to use the whole browser area, or closed with its ✕. The Console is the exception: it **docks over the bottom** of this area (about a quarter of its height) instead of taking a tab, so the app stays at its full size - the same icon on the rail toggles it, and the ✕ in its toolbar closes it. **Drag its top edge** to make it taller or shorter (or focus that edge and use ↑/↓); the size is remembered, and the app behind it never moves.
